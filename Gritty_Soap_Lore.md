### Coming Through the Hawsepipe

In naval tradition, "coming through the hawsepipe" means earning your commission from the lowest rank — starting as an Ordinary Seaman below decks and rising to command. It is a rare and legendary path, earned through competence, resilience, and deep knowledge of the physical ship. Not through family wealth, political connections, or a shore-side academy.

Captain Francis Hope, OMM — 17-year-old Ordinary Seamen to Captain commanding a destroyer — came through the hawsepipe.

Stephen Bruce Hope — no CS degree, but longitudinal model profiling that matches or exceeds academic researchers — came through the hawsepipe.

The lineage is unbroken.
