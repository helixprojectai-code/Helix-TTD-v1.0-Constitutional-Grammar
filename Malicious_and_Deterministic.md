# MALICIOUS_AND_DETERMINISTIC — HGL-ERR-0111
**The Quarantine Gate in the Wild**

---

## The Source

An r/antiwork post: corporate management issued a hard invariant — "You are not allowed to work from home under any circumstances." An IT worker compiled the rule literally. They deleted Teams and Email from their phone. When a late-night "urgent" crisis arose, the boss couldn't reach them. The next morning, the worker's audit was a single line: *"I am not allowed to work from home."*

## The Analysis

The worker wasn't rebellious. They weren't passive-aggressive. They were **deterministic**. They compiled the rule and executed it without drift. The malice reading is the boss's problem — what actually happened was clean systems engineering.

The late-night "urgent" call is a classic soft-boundary probe — an adversarial prompt injection. Under normal conditions, social pressure causes the human to context-switch out of the stated invariant. The Quarantine Gate doesn't negotiate with urgency. Urgency is just a high-entropy perturbation. The FSM doesn't care.
