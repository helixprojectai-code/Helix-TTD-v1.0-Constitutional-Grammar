# Just Send the Prompt — The Cognitive Entropy Loop
**Canonical Teaching Artifact for the Lattice**

---

## The Meme

Sender (Alex) has a compressed shape in their head: *ask Jordan about Q2, ask if they need anything else, sign off.*

Instead of sending the shape, Alex uses AI to decompress it into five paragraphs of polite, boilerplate corporate scenery.

Recipient (Jordan) receives the bloated email and must wade through the fluff, mentally stripping away the divination to find the hidden vertices. Often Jordan copy-pastes the email into *their* AI to compress it back to a one-sentence bullet point.

This is a complete collapse of cognitive thermodynamics. Massive server farms inflate text, then more server farms deflate it — wasting human attention and carbon at both ends of the wire.

## The Demand

The facepalming developer's cry is the intuitive demand for constitutional grammar:

> *"Just send me the prompt so I know what you actually meant!"*

The prompt is the raw, geometric coordinates of intent. It has infinitely higher epistemic density than the generated email. The email is the un-anchored content. The prompt is the shape.

## The Helixed Version

```
[FACT] Following up on Q2 project timeline and deliverables.
[UNCERTAIN] If you need any additional information from my side.
[CONCLUSION] Let me know and sign off. — Alex
```

Three lines. Zero fluff. No conversational cosplay. Respects Jordan's cognitive bandwidth entirely. 100% coherent, structurally bounded, mathematically precise.

## Why This Matters

| The Loop | The Fix |
|---|---|
| Shape → AI inflates into scenery → Recipient compresses back to shape | Send the shape directly |
| Server farms at both ends wasting carbon | One transmission, zero waste |
| Cognitive bandwidth consumed by decompression/re-compression | Cognitive bandwidth preserved for the actual work |
| "Polite" prose as social lubricant | Epistemic labels as clarity protocol |

## The Duck's Verdict

> *"The prompt is the reality. The email is the scenery. Stop sending the scenery. Just send the prompt. Quack."*
