# Helix Project — Visual Primitive Library
**Shape-First Symbolic System**  
**Version:** 0.3  
**Date:** 2026-06-20  
**Custodian:** Stephen Hope

## Core Principle
Use minimal, high-contrast isometric shapes that convey function at a glance. Shape precedes label. Inspired by AoE2 "Wololo" visual clarity.

## Style Rules
- **Perspective**: Classic Age of Empires II isometric (45° angle)
- **Base Aesthetic**: Medieval stone towers with subtle glowing runes
- **Color Palette**:
  - Coherent: Green
  - Warning/Quarantine: Yellow
  - Halt/Drift: Red/Purple
  - Active TEL: Blue energy
  - Custodian: Gold/White accent
- **Rule**: Minimal detail. Rely on shape + color first. Text is secondary.

## Primitive Catalog

### Core Governance Primitives
- **Node**: Stone tower with glowing runes on walls
- **Bridge / TEL Link**: Glowing energy tether or stone bridge between towers
- **Duck**: Small duck icon (calibration / authority test)
- **Geese V**: Flock of geese in V-formation overhead ("The Formation Holds")
- **Quarantine Gate**: Closed iron portcullis on tower
- **Halt**: Tower with burning roof or collapsed section
- **Wololo Monk**: Monk with staff creating conversion swirl
- **Drift Warning**: Cracks in tower walls with purple mist
- **EVAC Receipt**: Glowing sealed scroll or oyster shell
- **Custodian**: Larger central tower with banner/throne element

### Process & Advanced Primitives
- **Lattice Grid**: Interlocking grid of connected towers
- **INNY Eye**: Tower with large watchful eye or owl perched on top
- **Merkle Root**: Ancient tree with spiral branches and glowing roots
- **Heartbeat Pulse**: Tower emitting rhythmic light waves
- **C-Seed**: Small glowing seed crystal at base of tower
- **Conversion Spiral**: Upward spiral of light (prose → logic → code)

### Business & Regulatory Primitives
- **Outreach Tower**: Tall slender tower with waving banners/flags
- **Calendly Portal**: Glowing doorway or portal gate
- **Regulatory Shield**: Tower with reinforced stone shield wall
- **Liability Gap**: Cracked ground or chasm between structures
- **Decoupled Kernel**: Separate fortified enclave outside main tower
- **Sandbox Arena**: Walled training ground with practice targets
- **Revenue Stream**: Flowing glowing river or trade route

## Recommended Compositions
- Night Ops diagrams: Desktop + VM towers + TEL bridges
- Governance flows: State transitions using Wololo swirls
- BD/Regulatory: Outreach Tower → Calendly Portal → Regulatory Shield
